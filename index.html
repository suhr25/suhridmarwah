I am a B.Tech undergraduate in CS AI/ML based in Bangalore with a strong focus on systems engineering, robotics, and applied machine learning.

I contribute to open source projects. I have worked with organizations including Dora, OpenROAD, Rattler, MalariaGEN, Skforecast, CNCF, OpenTelemetry, and WasmEdge, contributing to real-world systems involving dataflow architectures, CI pipelines, and large-scale codebases.

My work centers on building reliable, testable systems that bridge intelligent agents with real or simulated environments. I have experience in Rust, C++, and Python, along with hands-on exposure to distributed systems, robotics simulations, and agent-based architectures. I also explored Arduino (C++) back in class 9th, sparking my early interest in hardware and embedded systems.
